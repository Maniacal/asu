# ASU Fall 2026 Application Plan for Arizona Residents

This plan outlines the steps, requirements, and schedule for your daughters to apply to Arizona State University (ASU) for Fall 2026 as Arizona residents. It covers admission requirements, application processes, financial aid, Arizona-specific programs, and a timeline to stay on track.

## 1. Admission Requirements
To be admitted to ASU as first-year students, your daughters must meet the following course competency and aptitude requirements, as outlined in your notes and ASU’s official guidelines.

### Course Competency Requirements
ASU requires specific high school coursework to ensure students are prepared for college-level academics:
- **Mathematics**: 4 years (Algebra I, Geometry, Algebra II, and one course requiring Algebra II as a prerequisite).
- **English**: 4 years (non-ESL/ELL courses).
- **Laboratory Sciences**: 3 years (1 year each from biology, chemistry, earth science, integrated sciences, or physics).
- **Social Sciences**: 2 years (including 1 year of American history).
- **Second Language**: 2 years (same language).
- **Fine Arts or Career and Technical Education**: 1 year.

**Action**: Verify with their high school counselor that their current and planned courses fulfill these requirements by the end of their senior year (spring 2026).

### Aptitude Requirements
ASU requires one of the following for admission:
- **Top 25%** of high school graduating class.
- **GPA**: 3.00 in competency courses (on a 4.00 = "A" scale).
- **ACT**: 22 (Arizona residents).
- **SAT**: 1120 (Arizona residents).

**Note**: ASU remains test-optional for Fall 2026 admissions, meaning ACT/SAT scores are not required but can be submitted for course placement or as supplemental information. Some competitive programs (e.g., Barrett, The Honors College) may recommend or require test scores.

**Action**: Monitor their GPA and class rank through high school. If they plan to submit ACT/SAT scores, register for tests early (e.g., spring 2025 or fall 2025) to allow time for retakes if needed. Scores can be sent to ASU using the Federal School Code: 001081.

**Source**: https://admission.asu.edu/apply/first-year/competency-requirements[](https://admission.asu.edu/apply/first-year)

### Additional Considerations
- **High School Equivalency**: If either daughter pursues a high school equivalency (HSE) like the College Credit Pathway, ASU accepts it as a high school diploma but may require additional coursework or ACT/SAT scores to meet competency and aptitude requirements unless they have 24 transferable post-high school credits.
- **Competitive Programs**: Programs like Barrett, The Honors College, or certain majors (e.g., engineering, nursing) may have higher requirements, such as a minimum GPA of 3.5 or specific test scores. Check program-specific requirements on ASU’s Degree Search: https://degrees.asu.edu/.

**Action**: Research their intended majors on ASU’s Degree Search to confirm any additional requirements. If interested in Barrett, review its application process: https://barretthonors.asu.edu/admissions.

## 2. Application Process
ASU accepts applications through its online portal or the Common Application. Only one application is needed, and there’s no preference between the two. Below are the steps to apply, based on your notes and ASU’s guidelines.

### Steps to Apply
1. **Complete the Application**:
   - Access the ASU application at https://webapp4.asu.edu/ugapp/ or the Common Application at https://www.commonapp.org/.
   - Select the desired major and campus (e.g., Tempe, Polytechnic, Downtown Phoenix, West, or ASU Online).
   - Provide personal information, academic history, and residency details to confirm Arizona resident status.
   - Self-report high school grades or request official transcripts from the high school (official transcripts are required post-admission).

2. **Pay the Application Fee**:
   - **Cost**: $50 for Arizona residents.
   - **Payment Options**: Credit card (Visa or Mastercard), eCheck via My ASU, or mail a check/money order to:
     ```
     Admission Services Applicant Processing
     Arizona State University
     PO Box 871004
     Tempe, AZ 85287-1004
     ```
   - **Fee Waiver**: If financial circumstances qualify, answer questions in the application to automatically receive a waiver (no separate form needed).

3. **Submit Supporting Documents**:
   - **Transcripts**: Self-reported grades are sufficient initially, but official high school transcripts with graduation date are required post-admission. Ensure the high school sends them directly to ASU.
   - **Test Scores (Optional)**: If submitting, request ACT or College Board (SAT) to send scores to ASU (Federal School Code: 001081).
   - **Additional Materials**: Some programs may require essays, letters of recommendation, or portfolios. Check program-specific requirements.

4. **Monitor Application Status**:
   - After submission, track progress via My ASU (https://my.asu.edu/). Decisions typically take 2–6 weeks once all materials are received.

**Source**: https://admission.asu.edu/apply/first-year/admission[](https://admission.asu.edu/apply/first-year)

### Application Tips
- **Apply Early**: Submitting by the priority admission date (Nov. 1, 2025) maximizes scholarship opportunities and ensures earlier decisions.
- **Common Application**: If applying to multiple universities, use the Common Application to streamline the process, as ASU is a member.
- **Accuracy**: Double-check self-reported grades and residency information to avoid delays.
- **Parent Access**: Set up parent/guest access via https://registrar.asu.edu/parent-guest-access to monitor application and enrollment progress.

**Action**: Create accounts on My ASU and/or Common Application in summer 2025. Gather transcripts and test scores (if applicable) early. Confirm Arizona residency status on the application to qualify for in-state tuition and programs.

## 3. Financial Aid and Scholarships
As Arizona residents, your daughters are eligible for federal, state, and ASU-specific financial aid, including scholarships, grants, and work-study. Completing the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) is critical to access these opportunities.

### FAFSA
- **Purpose**: Determines eligibility for federal grants (e.g., Pell Grants), loans, work-study, and some ASU scholarships.
- **ASU’s Federal School Code**: 001081.
- **Priority Filing Date**: February 1, 2025, for maximum need-based aid consideration (likely the same for 2026).
- **Application**: Available at https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa. Opens October 1, 2025, for the 2026–2027 academic year.
- **Eligibility**: Requires U.S. citizenship or eligible noncitizen status, a valid Social Security number, and demonstrated financial need.

**Action**: Gather financial documents (e.g., 2025 tax returns, W-2s) in fall 2025. Submit the FAFSA by February 1, 2026, to maximize aid.

**Source**: https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa[](https://admission.asu.edu/cost-aid)

### ASU Scholarships
- **New American University (NAMU) Scholarship**:
  - Merit-based for high-achieving students (e.g., top 25% of class, high GPA, or strong test scores).
  - Automatically considered upon admission; no separate application needed.
  - Award amounts vary based on academic performance.
- **Departmental Scholarships**: Offered by specific colleges or programs. Check eligibility on the ASU scholarship portal: https://scholarships.asu.edu/.
- **Priority Deadline**: Apply for admission by November 1, 2025, to be considered for the maximum scholarship opportunities.

**Action**: Ensure applications are submitted by November 1, 2025, to qualify for merit-based scholarships. Explore the ASU scholarship portal in fall 2025 for additional opportunities.

**Source**: https://admission.asu.edu/cost-aid[](https://admission.asu.edu/cost-aid)

### Arizona Resident Programs
Your notes highlight two key programs for Arizona residents, which can significantly reduce costs.

#### Arizona Promise Program
- **Overview**: Provides scholarships to cover tuition and fees for eligible Arizona residents pursuing a bachelor’s degree.
- **Eligibility**:
  - Arizona resident.
  - Graduate from an Arizona high school with a minimum 2.5 GPA.
  - Family adjusted gross income (AGI) not exceeding $80,000 (2025 limit; may adjust for 2026).
  - Enroll full-time at ASU, University of Arizona, or Northern Arizona University.
  - Complete the FAFSA and meet federal Pell Grant eligibility.
- **Application**: No separate application; eligibility determined via FAFSA and admission application.
- **Details**: https://tuition.asu.edu/special-financial-assistance-programs-arizona-residents#webspark-anchor-link--530

**Action**: Confirm family AGI and ensure FAFSA completion by February 1, 2026. Verify high school GPA with counselors.

#### College Attainment Grant Program
- **Overview**: Covers tuition and fees for Arizona residents at ASU, focusing on increasing college access.
- **Eligibility**:
  - Arizona resident.
  - Enroll full-time in an undergraduate program.
  - Meet financial need criteria (determined via FAFSA).
  - Specific GPA or academic requirements may apply (check ASU’s website closer to 2026).
- **Application**: No separate application; eligibility assessed through FAFSA and admission.
- **Details**: https://tuition.asu.edu/special-financial-assistance-programs-arizona-residents#webspark-anchor-link--523

**Action**: Submit FAFSA by February 1, 2026, and confirm eligibility criteria on ASU’s tuition website in spring 2025.
